33/* === Binary Operators ===
34
35Binary operators need for execution two operands.
36They are always written in infix notation.
37The operators can be grouped with parentheses:
38The two variants
39
40  <Evaulator><Operator><Evaluator>
41
42and
43
44  (<Evaulator><Operator><Evaluator>)
45
46have as standalone expressions precisely the same semantics.
47The parenthesis variant exists to override operator precedence:
48
49  2 + 3 * 4
50
51is evaluated to <em>2 + 12</em>, then finally <em>14</em>.
52
53  (2 + 3) * 4
54
55is evaluated to <em>5 * 4</em>, then finally <em>20</em>.
56
57The order of precedence is as follows, ordered weak to strong binding:
58* the ternary operator
59* logical disjunction
60* logical conjunction
61* equality, inequality
62* less, less-equal, greater, greater-equal
63* plus, binary minus
64* times, divided
65* logical negation
66* unary minus
67
68In the following, the operators are ordered by precedence, stronger binding last.
69*/
